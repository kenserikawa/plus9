# Plus 9

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS number puzzle: drag or tap tiles into the centre until they add up to the target exactly. No build step for local play, no dependencies, no package.json. Open `index.html` directly (file:// works). The page makes **no external requests** (fonts are bundled); web portals require this, so keep it that way. There is **no server**: all progress lives in `localStorage` (see *Saving*).

## Screens and modes

- **Loading screen** (`#loader`, `loader.js`): on the saved theme's background, Nine (dressed in the saved outfit) stands in a well; the ring's 9 segments light up in the tile colors as the scripts, the fonts and the game's start come in, and Nine's eyes follow the newest one. Then Nine cheers and flies to its place on the first screen while the loading screen fades; it lands with a hop. With reduced motion the whole screen just fades. No text (it shows before `i18n.js`), no tap to start.
- **Home** (`#home`): top bar with How to play (`?`) and Settings (gear; effects and music are switched there, or both at once with `M`), Journey card (world and next level, total stars), Daily / Classic / 60 seconds cards, today's missions, and the Customize card (Nine as dressed, current theme) that opens the `#style` sheet: Nine's wardrobe and the themes. A first-time player skips it and lands in Journey level 1 (`plus9.onboarded` is set when they win it). The first time home shows after that (`maybeStartTour` from `showScreen`), a **home tour** (`TOUR`, the `#tour` dialog) has Nine point at its parts one coach bubble at a time: the Journey card, the three mode cards, then missions and Customize, each ringed in gold (`.tour-target`). `placeTour` puts the bubble below the ringed parts, else above, else beside them (short landscape frames), with its arrow on that side. Next, Skip, Esc or a tap outside the bubble (moves on); closing it sets `plus9.tour`. Keyboard shortcuts are off while it is open.
- **Journey map** (`#map`): a winding route of levels (`renderMap`); finished levels show their stars, the next one is gold, later ones locked. Tapping a level starts it at once (no intro screen); "Next level" on the result does the same. A level's goal and star thresholds show as a coach tip when it starts (`coach.goal`), unless that level has a lesson tip. The route fades out at its top and bottom edges. `drawRoad()` draws an SVG under the nodes with an S-curve from each node's centre to the next: stretches after a level with a star are an ink-cased rainbow that slides along (SMIL `animateTransform`, off with reduced motion); the rest are dotted. It measures the nodes, so it reruns on resize. - **Game** (`#game`): bar (home, title, pause, sound; in the Journey the home button is a back arrow, `#i-back`, to the map centred on the level being left: `goBack` → `goMap` → `openMap(n)`, set by `renderBackButton`), HUD, coach tip, board, actions. The HUD's 2nd and 3rd pills change with the mode (`renderHud`): Level/Best (Classic, 60 s), Sums/Par (Daily), Stars/Sums (Journey).
- Starting any game calls `platform.commercialBreak()` first; `goHome()` abandons the current game (Classic/60 s records are kept, the daily keeps its own saved progress).

## Rules (as implemented)

- **Tiles**: 8 tiles, values 1…`target − 1`, weighted towards small numbers: P(v) ∝ exp(−v / λ), λ per level from `TILE_LAMBDA`. Playing a tile adds it to the well and the tile becomes the **Next** value. Four random colors (`COLORS`); each has a shape (`COLOR_IDS` in app.js: orange ▲, blue ●, purple ■, pink ◆, symbols `#mark-*`) drawn in the tile's top-left corner and on Next/queue tiles, but only shown with **Color shapes** on (settings, `plus9.shapes`, off by default; How to play points color-blind players to it). Wild, rainbow and frozen tiles have none. Tile `aria-label`s always say the color.
- **Scoring**: a completed sum scores `tiles used × level`, **×2 when every tile has the same color** (a combo, at least 2 tiles; wild tiles match any color), and ×2 more for each ×2 tile in it.
- **Special tiles** (`drawTile` rolls them; every draw consumes the roll so seeded sequences don't shift): **★ wild** (`WILD_CHANCE` 3 %) is worth exactly what the sum still needs (`tileValue`), so it always finishes it; **×2** (`DOUBLE_CHANCE` 6 %) doubles its sum's points; **rainbow** (`RAINBOW_CHANCE` 4 %) keeps its number but matches any color, so it never breaks a combo (`anyColor`, `keepsCombo`). Its digit is drawn twice: the ink-outlined number, and an `::after` copy (from `data-n`) filled with a rainbow gradient clipped to the glyph, about one glyph wide and sliding slowly (the one idle animation on a tile, stopped by reduced motion). A rainbow part fills the ring with cycling colors. **Frozen** tiles (`ice`, journey only) can't be played (`canPlay`) and thaw by one per completed sum (`ICE_SUMS` = 2); shuffles leave them in place. Journey only, by `mechanics` (`drawTile(lv, rnd, specials, mechanics)`; with it on every draw also rolls a size): **minus tiles** (`MINUS_CHANCE` 5 %, levels 81+) are worth −1…−5 (`MECHANIC_SIZE`); played to the centre they subtract, only if the sum stays at 0 or more (`canPlay(tile, need, sum)`), and they neither count as tiles (`tilesUsed`) nor have a color (`sumPoints`, `keepsCombo`); silver, `.is-minus`. **Couplers** (`COUPLER_CHANCE` 5 %, levels 91+) are worth +1…+5, never go to the centre, and fuse onto a plain, ×2 or rainbow tile that isn't frozen if the result stays within the target (`canFuse`, `fuse`): the tile keeps its color and special, the coupler's slot takes Next; bronze with a plug badge, `.is-coupler`. Stuck is `isStuck`: something in the centre and no tile can go in (a fusion can never help: the tile it fuses onto would already fit). Both have no color shape and read their face as −n / +n (`faceOf`).
- **Classic streak** (`streakOn()`, only `mode === 'classic'`): a completed sum goes into a **pot** instead of the score, the k-th sum in a row worth k × its points (`streakMultiplier`, capped at `STREAK_MAX` = 5). **Cash in** (`cashIn`, `#cash`, key `C`) moves the pot to the score and restarts the streak; it is allowed between sums only (`canCash`: sum 0, or a finished sum / level-up card still on show). Getting **stuck** busts the streak (`bust()`): the pot is lost, then the usual stuck choices apply. A pot still open at game over is lost (the result says so, `res.potLost`). The `#streak` row (multiplier for the next sum, `STREAK_MAX` pips, Cash in + pot) sits in `.actions`: its own row in portrait (`order: -1`), under Shuffle in landscape; `.game.has-streak` shrinks `--cell` so it fits. A coach tip explains it until the first cash-in (`plus9.cashed`). New players are pointed at it: the home Classic card shows **¡Nuevo! Rachas ×5** until Classic is played (`plus9.classicSeen`), and the first win of journey level 5 adds a tip to the result. Sound follows the streak: each sum's arpeggio starts higher (`sfx.complete(tiles, mult − 1)`), cashing in plays `sfx.cash(mult)`, a bust `sfx.bust()`. If a lost pot would have beaten the record, the result says so (`res.potRecord`). The score and the pot count up when they grow (`showCount`). Classic also shows the `QUEUE_SIZE` (2) tiles after Next (`queue`, `#queue`), redrawn at level-up. `tools/balance.js` checks the decision matters: cashing at ×3–×5 beats cashing every sum and never cashing.
- **Levels** (Classic, 60 s, Daily): every `SUMS_PER_LEVEL` (4) sums, no top level. Target follows `TARGETS` (9 … 20), then +2 per level. The `#levelup` card shows the old target struck through and the new one.
- **Shuffle** rerolls all 8 tiles (key `S`). Free shuffles: 1 at the start, +1 per level-up while under 3. Otherwise it costs `SHUFFLE_COST × level × (paid so far + 1)` points.
- **Losing**: going over the target ends the game, except that once per game (not in the Daily, only with `platform.hasRewarded`) `offerRevive` first shows the result sheet as **¡Por poco!** with **End game** and **🎬 Take it back** (`#over-reward`): the ad runs after the sheet closes, and watching it takes the tile back (it never left the board, so only `sum` drops). **Stuck** (sum above 0, no tile fits) blocks play until the player acts: free shuffle, pay, **Bank** (key `B`: end and keep the points; in Spanish **Plantarse**, since **Cobrar** is the Classic cash-in; in the Journey it reads **Give up**) or **🎬 Free shuffle** when `platform.hasRewarded`. With none of those available the game ends.
- **Records**: the final score, per mode (`plus9.best.classic`, `plus9.best.timed`), so points spent on shuffles count against it. The result sheet compares the score with the previous record.
- **Journey** (`journeyLevel(n)`): a fixed seeded board; make the target `sums` times at a fixed difficulty (no level-ups). Special tiles are off until level 6 (`specials`). `setup` (applied by `applySetup` in both the game and the bot) hands out a wild tile on level 6, a ×2 on level 7, a rainbow tile on level 8, a minus tile (−3) on level 81 and a coupler (+3) on level 91 (`setup` may set a `value`), and freezes two tiles on the 1st, 3rd and 5th level of every tier from level 11. Coach tips introduce each one (6, 7, 8, 11, 81, 91) with a highlighted tile (frozen ones are never ringed). `mechanics` per level: 0 up to 80, 1 (minus tiles) 81–90, 2 (both) from 91. Tier = every 5 levels up to level 80 (target 9, 10, 12, 14 … 38); within a tier sums go 3 → 7. Stars: 1★ finishing, 2★ and 3★ at score thresholds from `journeyStars` (2★ ≈ a typical player, 3★ ≈ the careful bot). Level 1 starts from a hand-picked board. Levels 1–4 show coach tips (`tutorial()`): level 1 walks through the first sum with a highlighted tile; 2 = Next, 3 = color combos, 4 = shuffle. `JOURNEY_SEEDS` (levels 1–200) were chosen by `tools/journey.js` so typical players win ~95 % at level 1 down to 40 % (bosses a notch lower, down to 33 %), then stay there. From level 81 the numbers stop growing (`JOURNEY_PEAK_TIER` = 15, target 38, as levels 76–80): a sum needs a tile worth exactly what is missing, and with bigger targets that is ever less likely, so beyond ~95 no board came near the goal and level 83 was all but unwinnable. Sums, bosses and ice keep cycling. Past level 200, `journeySeedIndex` reuses the seeds from 81 on in a 120-level cycle (a multiple of 10, so bosses and ice line up). Seeds 81–200 were picked with minus tiles and couplers (typical players 31–43 %), and every level 81–200 has a winning line (`node tools/solve.js 81-200`). Difficulty past the plateau comes from those mechanics in worlds 9 and 10, not from bigger numbers.
- **Daily** (`mode === 'daily'`): same tiles for everyone on a local date (`createRng(hashSeed(todayKey()))`). **All randomness goes through `rng`; never call `Math.random` directly for game draws.** `DAILY_SUMS` (12) sums, one attempt. Progress is saved on every `render()` (`plus9.daily`), so reloading resumes instead of rerolling. **Par** = what the careful bot scores on today's board (`todaysPar()`); shown when the daily starts, in the HUD and in the result and share text. Result: 2×6 emoji grid (🟦 2 tiles, 🟪 3, 🟨 4+, 💥 bust, ⬜ unplayed), streak (`plus9.streak`), countdown, Share (`navigator.share` or clipboard, URL from `platform.shareURL()`). Daily #1 is `DAILY_EPOCH`: **set it to the launch date before publishing**.
- **Missions** (`meta.MISSIONS`): 3 a day, the same for everyone (seeded by the date). Events from `app.js` (`meta.missionEvent`) advance them; each completed mission adds a star to `plus9.wallet` and shows a toast.
- **Stars**: total = journey stars + mission stars + bonus stars (`plus9.wallet.bonusStars`): a journey win with stars the level never had before offers **🎬 +N ★** on the result sheet (`doubleStars`), doubling only those new stars so replays can't farm them. Stars buy the star-priced wardrobe items.
- **Worlds** (`WORLDS`, `WORLD_SIZE` = 10 in rules.js): grape, sunset, ocean, night, candy, forest, space, neon, chrome (minus tiles), gears (couplers); past level 100 the journey stays in gears. The map shows a banner where each world starts; each world's last level is a **boss** (`isBoss`: 8 sums, and from level 20 three frozen tiles; title "Boss: level n", crown on the map). Beating a boss for the first time completes the world (`completeWorld`): Nine gets and wears the world's item, the next world's theme switches on, and the result sheet shows the prize (`#over-prize`).
- **Themes** (`meta.THEMES`) are the worlds: a world's theme (background `--bg` and music style) unlocks when the player reaches it (`meta.worldReached`) and is chosen in the Customize sheet.
- **Nine's wardrobe** (`meta.WARDROBE`): one item per completed world (party hat, sunglasses, diving mask, wizard hat, bow tie, explorer hat, antennae, headphones) and three bought with total stars (cap 15, scarf 30, crown 60). One item per slot (head, face, neck), saved in `plus9.outfit`. Locked items show as grey previews with how to get them. `announceNewItems()` toasts items unlocked since the last check (`knownItems`).
- **Pause**: the settings sheet `#settings` is the pause menu in a game (`Esc`, the pause button): title Pause, Resume, How to play, Home (Map in the Journey); on the home screen (gear) it is Settings with Done. How to play (`#help`, also `?` on home and `H`/`?`) opened from it or mid-game returns to it with Back (`helpFromSettings`), so the game stays paused until Resume. `paused()` = either sheet open; it pauses the 60-second clock, and so does hiding the page. `M` mutes.

## Saving

Everything is `localStorage` under `plus9.*` (each access in try/catch): `best.classic`, `best.timed`, `daily`, `streak`, `journey`, `missions`, `wallet`, `theme`, `outfit`, `effects`, `music` (`muted` only in old saves), `shapes`, `onboarded`, `tour`, `cashed`, `classicSeen`, `lang`. No backend. On Poki, a logged-in player's `localStorage` is synced to their account automatically by the SDK (restored across devices, 1 MB limit); keys starting with `poki_ignore` are excluded. Keep saves small and in `localStorage` so that keeps working.

## Balance

`node tools/balance.js` (rules.js + bot.js): normal player's dead-end rate ≈16 % at level 1 rising ~2 points per level; whole games: normal ≈27 sums, careful ≈125, none endless (wild tiles soften bad luck). The streak table (`simulateRun({ bankAt })`): median Classic score by when the bot cashes in; normal ≈320 cashing every sum vs ≈450 at ×3–×5, careful ≈4500 vs ≈8900. Run it before and after touching `TARGETS`, `TILE_LAMBDA`, `SUMS_PER_LEVEL`, `MAX_FREE_SHUFFLES`, `SHUFFLE_COST`, `COMBO_MULTIPLIER` or `STREAK_MAX`. If the journey's rules change, rerun `node tools/journey.js` and update `JOURNEY_SEEDS`.

## Portals (Poki first, CrazyGames later)

`platform.js` hides each SDK behind `init`, `loadingFinished`, `gameplayStart`, `gameplayStop`, `commercialBreak()` → Promise, `rewardedBreak()` → Promise<boolean>, `shareURL()`, `hasRewarded`, `adShowing`. `window.PLUS9_PLATFORM` picks the SDK (`'poki'`); unset means `'none'`: no-ops and **no rewarded ads** (a self-hosted copy would otherwise hand out unlimited free shuffles). `?testads` simulates 1.2 s ads locally.

The adapter enforces Poki's rules (no repeated start/stop, no events during ads, ad promises always resolve). `app.js` calls `loadingFinished()` once the SDK, the portal's language and the fonts are in (before the loading screen leaves); `gameplayStart()` on the first input; `gameplayStop()` on game over, level-up, pause and leaving; `commercialBreak()` when starting a game, after the level-up card and when unpausing mid-game; `rewardedBreak()` only from 🎬 (free shuffle while stuck, take-back after going over, +N ★ on a journey win). During an ad: audio suspended, clock paused, `body.is-ad` blocks input.

Poki UI rules followed: 16:9 frames (640×360, 836×470, 1031×580) use the landscape layout; the 🎬 button is never green, sits beside or after the standard choices and is never larger or animated; no external links or requests; arrow keys, space and the wheel don't scroll the host page except inside `.sheet` and `.scroll` (the map).

**Build**: `node tools/build.js poki` adds `<script src="https://game-cdn.poki.com/scripts/v2/poki-sdk.js">` (no SRI: Poki updates that file in place) and zips the package. Verified over HTTP with the real SDK: it loads, and the event order is start → stop (level end) → commercialBreak (next start) → start → stop (pause) → commercialBreak (resume). **Still to do**: upload to Poki for Developers, run Poki Inspector, set `DAILY_EPOCH` to the launch date. The animated thumbnail, screenshots and listing texts are in `promo/`.

## Conventions

- **Input** goes through `play(tile)`: `click` for tap/keyboard, Pointer Events with a `.ghost` clone for drag; the click after a drag is suppressed. Drag writes the ghost's `translate3d` once per animation frame, eases its tilt from horizontal speed, and hit-tests a circular `dropZone()` measured at drag start (never the live well rect: the well scales up under a tile, and measuring that made it flicker at the edge). On release the ghost slides into the well or back home (`land`; `done` always clears `lifted`, also when reduced motion skips the slide). A coupler is picked up instead (`holdCoupler`: tap, Enter or the start of a drag): `markFusion` rings the tiles it can fuse onto and dims the rest, `holding` keeps it until a target is tapped (`fuseInto`), the coupler is tapped again or Esc (`dropHold`); dragging hit-tests each target's circle (`zoneOf`) measured at drag start, and a drop on the well sends it home with a tip. A held coupler is dropped whenever the game moves on (new game, shuffle, sum completed, stuck, pause, leaving).
- **Ring**: one SVG segment per unit of the target, coloured by the tile that filled it.
- **Nine** sits in the actions row (top of the actions column in landscape) and is the "9" of the home title. Moods: hop on every tile, happy on a sum, cheer on combos / ×2 / level-ups / wins / new records (with confetti), worried while stuck, sad on a loss. Confetti also marks the tutorial's first sum. Confetti skips with reduced motion; the mascot's only idle motion is a blink every few seconds.
- **Poking Nine**: on the home screen (title) and in Customize, Nine is a `.nine-poke` button (`pokeNine`). Pokes cycle love (hearts), spin, wink (star), boing; five pokes within two seconds make it dizzy (spiral eyes, wobble). Each has a sound (`sfx.poke(kind)`) and floating symbols (`.poke-bit`, skipped with reduced motion). Its open pupils follow the pointer or last touch there (`mascot.look`), never during play.
- **Motion** only answers the player (pop-in, match, shake, bump, floating points, level-up card, result stars). The only loop is the Shuffle `nudge` while stuck and payable. Tutorial hints are a steady gold ring, not an animation. The loading screen is the one entrance (the ring filling, Nine's flight). `prefers-reduced-motion` turns motion off (the loading screen fades instead).
- **Numbers** on tiles, Next and help icons are outlined with 16 unblurred `text-shadow`s (radius `--o`), not `-webkit-text-stroke`.
- **The target is always prominent**: an empty well shows it big in gold under "Make".
- **Labels** on pills and buttons use `--label-font`/`--label-size` (Ranchers 400), ink on white. Baloo 2 is for running text.
- **Icons** are `<symbol>`s in `index.html` used via `<use>`; CSS can't reach inside them, so state changes swap the symbol (`#i-sound-on` / `#i-sound-off`) instead of styling inner paths.
- **Layout**: `--cell` sizes the board. Portrait is one column (max 460 px); landscape-and-short switches the game to three columns (info | board | actions) and home to two. Check 375×667, ~1000×780, 640×360, 836×470 and 1031×580 after layout changes; none may scroll except the map route.
- **Ids**: the result sheet has both `#over-reward` (the 🎬 revive / double-stars button) and `#over-prize` (world prize); check `index.html` for an existing id before adding one.
- **Result buttons**: the two choices take half the row each, or stack at full width when a label doesn't fit in half, so they always match. `#over-reward` sits after them on its own row (beside them in short landscape frames, where the sheet is wider and compact so 🎬 is never below the fold), at its own size, never larger.
- **Onboarding**: every new player-facing feature ships with its teaching, in the game's own coach system (no tour libraries such as driver.js: the game teaches by playing, in its own look, with no extra dependency): a coach tip the first time it matters, the gold `hint` ring on the element when it helps, a `plus9.*` flag when it should show only once (like `cashed`, `classicSeen`), the text in all six languages, and a line in How to play. Something new on the home screen gets a **New!** badge until it is used, and a step in the home tour (`TOUR`) for players who have not seen it yet. Note where it is taught in this file. Taught so far: minus tiles at level 81 and couplers at level 91 (coach tip + ring on the handed-out tile), their How to play lines (`#rule-minus`, `#rule-coupler`, shown once the world is reached), and the tips for a minus tile the sum can't afford, a coupler dropped in the middle and a coupler with no tile to fuse onto.
- **Accessibility**: screens are `<main>` (one shown at a time) with an `<h1>` that takes focus when the screen changes (`showScreen`); dialogs are named by their title; never convey something by color alone (see Color shapes); keyboard shortcuts that open a dialog consume their key (`preventDefault`), or the browser closes the dialog with the same Esc.
- **Text**: never hard-code player-facing words in app.js or index.html; add a key to every language in `i18n.js` (keep the six dictionaries in step; a quick Node check compares their keys). Game-over reasons are passed as `{ k, v }` and translated when shown (`say`), so a saved daily result follows the current language. Check the longest languages (de, fr) at 375 px after UI changes. French uses no-break spaces before ! ? : ;.
- **Style**: 4-space indent, no semicolons, `const`/`let`, no implicit globals; `localStorage` always in try/catch; set user-facing text with `textContent`.
