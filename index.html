        How to play

        • 4

          Hit the number exactly. Drag or tap tiles into the middle until they add up to the target.

        • +

          Long chains, one color. A sum is worth the tiles you used times your level. All one color? Double points.

        • ★

          Special tiles. A ★ wild tile finishes any sum. A ×2 tile doubles the points of its sum. Frozen tiles thaw after 2 sums.

        • 6

          Plan with Next. It's the number that replaces the tile you play.

        • !

          Don't go over. If no tile fits, shuffle: free ones first, then you pay points. You can also bank your points and stop, or watch an ad 🎬 where offered.

        • ⚑

          Journey. Beat levels for up to 3 stars. Stars and daily missions unlock themes.

        • 1

          Daily, Classic, 60 seconds. The same board for everyone each day; an endless game where the target keeps growing; a race against the clock.
