How to play

  • 4

    Hit the number exactly. Drag or tap tiles into the middle until they add up to the target. It starts at 9.

  • +

    Longer chains score more. Each sum is worth the tiles you used times your level, so 2 + 3 + 4 beats 8 + 1.

  • 6

    Plan with Next. It shows the number that replaces the tile you play.

  • !

    Don't go over. Too much ends the game. If no tile fits, you have to shuffle.

  • ⟳

    Shuffle rerolls the board. You get a free one at the start and one more each level. With none left, a shuffle costs points, and each one you pay for costs more.

  • ↑

    Level up every 4 sums. The target and the tiles get bigger.

  • 60

    60 seconds mode. Race the clock. Every sum adds 2 seconds.

Keys: Tab + Enter to play a tile, S shuffle, M sound, H help.
